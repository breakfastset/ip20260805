# SWIFTDRIVE RENTALS – TERMS AND CONDITIONS

Welcome to SwiftDrive Rentals.
Please read these Terms and Conditions carefully before renting a vehicle.
By signing your rental agreement, you agree to all terms listed below.

### 1. Driver Eligibility and Requirements
* **Minimum Age:** Drivers must be at least 21 years old.
Drivers between 21 and 24 years old are subject to a daily Young Driver Fee.
* **Driver's License:** The primary driver and all authorized additional drivers must present a valid, full driver’s license held for at least one consecutive year.
* **International Renters:** Renters with a license issued outside the country must provide a valid passport.
An International Driving Permit (IDP) is required if the license is not written in English or the Latin alphabet.

### 2. Rental Period, Late Returns, and Extensions
* **Rental Duration:** The rental period begins and ends at the exact dates and times specified in your reservation agreement.
* **Late Return Policy:** A grace period of 59 minutes is granted for returns.
Vehicles returned 60 minutes or more past the scheduled time will incur a full additional day's rental charge plus applicable taxes.
* **Rental Extensions:** If you need to extend your rental period, you must notify SwiftDrive Rentals at least 6 hours prior to your scheduled return time.
Extensions are subject to vehicle availability and current rate changes.

### 3. Payment, Fees, and Security Deposit
* **Forms of Payment:** SwiftDrive Rentals accepts major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) issued in the primary driver's name.
Debit cards and cash are not accepted at the time of pickup.
* **Security Deposit:** A temporary authorization hold of $200.00 will be placed on your credit card upon vehicle pickup.
This hold will be released following the safe, on-time return of the vehicle in its original condition.
* **Incidental Charges:** The renter is solely responsible for all traffic violations, parking tickets, speeding fines, and toll fees incurred during the rental period, along with a standard administrative processing fee per violation.

### 4. Insurance, Liability, and Coverage Exclusions
* **Included Coverage:** A basic Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) is included in the daily rental rate, capping your financial responsibility for vehicle damage.
* **Deductible:** In the event of damage to or theft of the vehicle, the renter is liable for a maximum deductible of $500.00.
* **Voiding of Coverage:** All insurance coverages, protections, and waivers are immediately rendered void if the vehicle is driven by an unauthorized driver, or if the driver is found to be operating the vehicle under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or medication that impairs driving ability.

### 5. Vehicle Use Restrictions
* **Prohibited Activities:** Vehicles may not be used for towing, pushing other vehicles, off-road driving, racing, commercial transport of goods or passengers (e.g., ridesharing), or any illegal activities.
* **Strict Non-Smoking Policy:** Smoking, vaping, and the use of tobacco products are strictly prohibited inside all SwiftDrive vehicles.
A specialized cleaning and detailing fee of $150.00 will be automatically assessed if any evidence of smoking is detected.
* **Pet Policy:** Pets are permitted only if secured inside an appropriate pet carrier.
Renters are responsible for removing pet hair, and excessive mess will result in additional cleaning fees.

### 6. Fuel and Vehicle Return Condition
* **Fuel Policy:** Vehicles are provided with a specific fuel level (typically a full tank) and must be returned with that exact same level of fuel.
* **Refueling Charges:** If the vehicle is returned with less fuel than at pickup, the renter will be billed for the missing fuel at local market prices, plus a $30.00 refueling service fee.
* **Return Cleanliness:** Vehicles must be returned in a clean and acceptable condition.
SwiftDrive Rentals reserves the right to charge an extra detailing fee for vehicles returned with excessive interior dirt, mud, stains, or trash.
